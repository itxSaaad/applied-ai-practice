# Incident communications

## Status update format

Write a short update containing customer impact, affected services, current mitigation, action owner, and the next update time. Use confirmed facts and label hypotheses. Do not copy secrets or personal data into incident channels.

## Timeline

Record timestamped observations, decisions, actions, owners, and measured outcomes. Keep the timeline append-only during response; reconcile it during the post-incident review.

## Resolution and review

Declare resolution only after representative user flows and service indicators recover for the agreed observation window. The review should cover impact, timeline, contributing factors, effective mitigations, and corrective actions with owners.
